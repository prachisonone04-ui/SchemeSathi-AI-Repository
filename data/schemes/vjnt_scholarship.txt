SCHEME NAME: Post-Matric Scholarship To VJNT Students - Maharashtra

STATE: Maharashtra
CATEGORY: VJNT

OBJECTIVE:
Financial assistance for VJNT students pursuing post-matric education.

ELIGIBILITY:
1. Applicant must belong to VJNT category.
2. Applicant must be a resident of Maharashtra.
3. Annual family income must not exceed Rs. 1.5 lakh.
4. Applicant must be enrolled in an approved post-matric course.
5. Minimum 75% attendance is required.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
1. Caste Certificate.
2. Income Certificate.
3. Caste Validity Certificate (if applicable).
4. Previous Year Marksheet.
5. Gap Certificate (if applicable).
6. Ration Card.
7. Leaving Certificate.
8. Parents Declaration.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit MahaDBT portal.
2. Login or register.
3. Select VJNT Post-Matric Scholarship.
4. Fill the application form.
5. Upload required documents.
6. Submit the application.
7. Save the application reference number.
