
SCHEME NAME: PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

CATEGORY: Farmer Welfare

OBJECTIVE:
To provide income support to eligible landholding farmer families in India.

BENEFIT:
Eligible farmer families receive financial assistance of Rs. 6000 per year in three installments, subject to scheme rules.

ELIGIBILITY:
1. Eligible landholding farmer families as defined by the scheme.
2. Certain institutional landholders and specified excluded categories are not eligible.
3. Eligibility must be verified through official government records.

DOCUMENTS:
1. Aadhaar Card.
2. Land ownership records.
3. Bank account details.
4. Other documents required by the official portal.

APPLICATION:
1. Visit the official PM-KISAN portal.
2. Complete Farmer Registration.
3. Enter Aadhaar and bank details.
4. Submit the required information.
5. Check application status on the portal.

OFFICIAL SOURCE:
https://pmkisan.gov.in/
