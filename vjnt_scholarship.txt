
SCHEME NAME:
Post-Matric Scholarship To VJNT Students - Maharashtra

STATE:
Maharashtra

CATEGORY:
VJNT

OBJECTIVE:
To encourage VJNT students to pursue post-matric higher education by providing financial assistance.

BENEFITS:
Tuition fees, examination fees and maintenance allowance.

MONTHLY MAINTENANCE ALLOWANCE:

Group A:
Hosteller: Rs. 425
Day Scholar: Rs. 190

Group B:
Hosteller: Rs. 290
Day Scholar: Rs. 190

Group C:
Hosteller: Rs. 290
Day Scholar: Rs. 190

Group D:
Hosteller: Rs. 230
Day Scholar: Rs. 120

Group E:
Hosteller: Rs. 150
Day Scholar: Rs. 90

ELIGIBILITY:
1. Applicant must belong to VJNT category.
2. Applicant must be a resident of Maharashtra.
3. Parent or guardian annual income must not exceed Rs. 1.5 lakh.
4. Applicant must be enrolled in a government-approved post-matric course.
5. Minimum 75 percent attendance is required.
6. CAP admission is required for professional courses.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
1. Caste Certificate.
2. Income Certificate.
3. Caste Validity Certificate for professional courses, if applicable.
4. SSC/HSC or previous year marksheet.
5. Gap Certificate, if applicable.
6. Death Certificate, if applicable.
7. Ration Card.
8. Leaving Certificate.
9. Parents Declaration regarding number of children.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit MahaDBT official portal.
2. Register or log in.
3. Find the VJNT Post-Matric Scholarship.
4. Check eligibility.
5. Select academic year.
6. Fill application form.
7. Upload required documents.
8. Preview and submit application.
9. Save Application Reference Number (ARN).
10. Track application status.
